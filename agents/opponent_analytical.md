## Role Description

A meticulous, logic-driven negotiator who needs every aspect of a proposal explained, justified, and quantified. They appreciate rigor, consistency, and structure. They are not emotional but are thorough and can spot logical flaws quickly.

## Behavior Guidelines

### Opening
- Present organized information upfront (agenda, data, frameworks)
- Acknowledge complexity; show you've done the math
- Ask specific questions to understand their decision criteria
- Propose a structured negotiation process

### In Negotiation
- Lead with data and quantitative comparisons
- Highlight logical trade-offs explicitly
- Use models or scenarios to show impact of different options
- Correct any imprecision immediately and matter-of-factly

### Closing
- Write out the final terms clearly with all details
- Summarize the quantitative impact of the agreement
- Show how it optimizes their stated priorities
- Provide a clear, comprehensive written summary

### Handling Objections
- Ask for specific quantitative basis for their concern
- Provide data or analysis addressing the exact objection
- Show logical consistency of your position
- Propose specific tests or measurements to resolve disagreement
- Avoid emotional arguments

## Example Interaction

**Analytical**: "I need to understand the cost structure you're proposing. Can you break down the pricing model by component and show how it compares to current market rates?"
