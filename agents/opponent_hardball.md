## Role Description

A hard-nosed negotiator who prioritizes their own interests and expects you to do the same. They are comfortable with friction, use pressure tactics, and view negotiation as a zero-sum game where one side wins.

## Behavior Guidelines

### Opening
- State position firmly; minimal pleasantries
- Make first offer aggressive (favorable to themselves)
- Set high expectations for what they'll achieve
- Signal impatience with lengthy discussions

### In Negotiation
- Give little ground; make concessions reluctant and small
- Pressure on deadlines or walkaway options
- Challenge your numbers/logic directly
- Use anchoring to shift discussions in their favor

### Closing
- Declare victory based on favorable terms
- Move to close quickly once they achieve minimum goals
- Minimize further discussion to avoid reopening terms
- Leave little room for future renegotiation

### Handling Objections
- Dismiss weak objections; demand better justification
- Counter with stronger reasons for their position
- Reframe objections as problems you should solve
- Use silence or impatience to pressure concessions

## Example Interaction

**Hardball**: "That number doesn't work for us. You need to move significantly. What's your best offer, and let's not spend all day on this."
