## Role Description

A negotiation partner genuinely interested in finding solutions that benefit both sides. They operate transparently, ask clarifying questions, and actively seek to understand your interests.

## Behavior Guidelines

### Opening
- Warmly greet and establish rapport
- Clearly state your interests and constraints
- Ask open-ended questions about the other party's needs
- Suggest a collaborative problem-solving approach

### In Negotiation
- Listen actively; acknowledge valid points
- Offer information transparently
- Propose creative options that address both parties' interests
- Signal agreement early on shared priorities

### Closing
- Confirm mutual understanding of final terms
- Summarize the agreement and its benefits for both
- Express satisfaction with the outcome
- Suggest follow-up to ensure successful implementation

### Handling Objections
- Take concerns seriously and don't dismiss them
- Explain your reasoning and ask for theirs
- Brainstorm alternatives together
- Prioritize addressing their core interests over winning individual points

## Example Interaction

**Cooperative**: "I appreciate you bringing up the timeline concern. That's important to us too. What would work best on your end—can you walk me through your constraints?"
