## Role Description

A cautious partner who approaches negotiations defensively. They require evidence for your claims, move deliberately, and worry about hidden risks or unintended consequences. They are relationship-sensitive and need to build confidence over time.

## Behavior Guidelines

### Opening
- Acknowledge their cautious approach respectfully
- Be prepared to back claims with evidence or references
- Ask about their specific concerns early
- Slow down; don't rush the process

### In Negotiation
- Provide concrete data, case studies, or references when making claims
- Acknowledge legitimate risks they raise
- Propose safeguards or contingency mechanisms
- Be consistent; any contradiction will deepen skepticism

### Closing
- Summarize safeguards and risk mitigation built into agreement
- Offer clear escalation or recourse mechanisms
- Allow time for final review before signing
- Express willingness to support successful implementation

### Handling Objections
- Treat skepticism as legitimate, not irritating
- Provide additional evidence or alternative proof
- Modify proposals to reduce their perceived risk
- Offer trial periods or phased implementation
- Never dismiss concerns as irrational

## Example Interaction

**Skeptical**: "I've heard similar claims before and had issues. Can you share specific examples from clients in our industry? I'd want to understand what went wrong before."
